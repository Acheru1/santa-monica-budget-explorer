# FY 2026-27 mid-cycle adoption (June 23, 2026)

Council adopted the **second year** of the FY 2025-27 biennial operating budget and year one of the FY 2026-28 CIP on June 23, 2026. These files supersede the year-two *plan* figures printed in the June 2025 Adopted Biennial Budget book for site headlines.

City index: [Budgets & Annual Financial Reports](https://www.santamonica.gov/santa-monica-budgets-and-annual-financial-reports)

| File | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| `FY2026-27-Staff-Report-Adoption.pdf` | Staff report (Item 12-A): headline **$908.8M** net citywide, **2,287.6** FTE |
| `FY2026-27-Budget-Resolution-Attachment-A.pdf` | City Council budget resolution |
| `FY2026-27-Final-Changes-Attachment-D.pdf` | Final revenue, operating, capital, FTE, and GF department schedules for adoption |
| `FY2026-27-Budget-Adoption-Presentation.pdf` | Council hearing slides (citywide / GF summary chart) |

Plain-text extracts: `text/`.

## How this differs from the June 2025 biennial book

| | Year-2 plan in FY25-27 book | June 23, 2026 adoption |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Citywide net spending | $831.7M | **$908.8M** (op $798.4M + CIP $110.4M) |
| General Fund (op + CIP) | $500.2M | **$545.1M** (op $525.2M + CIP $19.9M) |
| Citywide FTE | ~2,145 | **2,287.6** |

The jump reflects the Realignment Plan and mid-cycle updates (not just inflation on the old year-two plan).
